Privacy Policy for Tru Job Tracker

Last Updated: January 16, 2026

Introduction

Tru ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Job details (titles, customer names, budgets, due dates)
  • Expense records (descriptions, amounts, categories)
  • Photos and receipts (if you choose to upload them)

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, crash reports)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app functionality
  • Store your job and expense data locally on your device
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All your job and expense data is stored locally on your device
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not currently sync your data to cloud servers
  • Your Control: You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing purposes

We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes only.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (it's stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (by uninstalling the app or clearing app data)
  • Request information about our data practices

Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. Since data is stored locally on your device, your device's security features (passcode, biometric authentication) help protect your information.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via GitHub: github.com/blueline742/Tru
