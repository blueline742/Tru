**Stay on top of your jobs and budgets with Tru**

Tru is the simple job tracking app built specifically for tradespeople, contractors, and small business owners. Stop losing money on jobs that go over budget and keep all your job information in one place.

**KEY FEATURES**

📋 Job Management
• Create and track unlimited jobs
• Set budgets and monitor spending in real-time
• Track job status (Active, Completed, Cancelled)
• Set due dates and get visual alerts for urgent jobs
• Store customer names and job details

💰 Expense Tracking
• Log materials, labor, and miscellaneous expenses
• Attach receipt photos to expenses
• Instant budget calculations
• See at-a-glance if you're over or under budget
• Delete or edit expenses as needed

📊 Visual Progress Tracking
• Live progress bars show budget usage
• Color-coded alerts for jobs over budget
• Quick stats showing spent vs budgeted amounts
• Filter jobs by status (Over Budget, Due Soon)

📸 Receipt Management
• Take photos of receipts directly in the app
• Store receipt photos with each expense
• Never lose track of your paperwork

🔍 Search & Filter
• Quickly find jobs by name or customer
• Filter by budget status
• Show only urgent jobs due soon
• Organize your workload efficiently

📤 Share & Export
• Export job summaries with complete breakdown
• Share via text, email, or any app
• Perfect for invoicing or record keeping
• Professional formatted reports

✨ Simple & Fast
• No account required - works offline
• Lightning fast performance
• Clean, modern interface
• Built by tradies, for tradies

**WHO IS THIS FOR?**

Tru is perfect for:

• Small business owners managing multiple jobs
